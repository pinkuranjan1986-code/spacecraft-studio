WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
A standalone React 19 / TypeScript / Vite / Tailwind CSS web app for draft building-plan and interior concepts. It supports home, office, warehouse/godown, shop/commercial, custom and interior-only briefs. It has a five-step wizard, SVG floor layouts, editable room requirements and regeneration, optional Vastu notes, interior palettes, saved projects and versions, SVG/JSON exports, JSON import, print/PDF, consent-based consultation records and illustrative locality pages.

DEFAULTS TO PRESERVE
- No API keys or accounts are needed. Local demo projects and leads stay in browser localStorage.
- Supabase is optional. Keep blank placeholder variables in .env.example; do not invent credentials or contact numbers. Cloud Auth/Storage/API still need integration tests against a configured account.
- The layout engine is deterministic. Do not add Gemini or another AI service for room placement, geometry or dimensions.
- Keep the future AIProvider interface disabled unless explicitly requested. Future integrations must be server-side and require separate explicit customer consent before sharing plans/personal information. Consultation consent is not AI-sharing consent.
- Keep Vastu rules editable in src/config.ts. They are preference checks, not certification or universal compliance.
- All outputs must retain: Draft concept — professional review required.
- Rectangular concepts, including confirmed inscribed envelopes for irregular plots, are approximations. Do not claim construction readiness, structural safety, code approval, verified access/fire safety, costs or legally checked setbacks.
- Mood SVGs are illustrations, not measured furniture plans. Service areas are illustrative; do not invent local business addresses, reviews or completed project results.
- Preserve RLS, ownership restrictions and immutable project versions. Never put service-role, AI or payment secrets in frontend variables.
- Do not deploy, create paid resources, add payments or send customer data externally without an explicit user request.
